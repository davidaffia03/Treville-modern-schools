Meet the Founder

Mrs. Aniekan Affia brings to Treville Modern Schools a rare blend of legal precision, administrative expertise, and pedagogical insight. She holds a Masters Degree in Public Administration , A Bachelor Of Laws Degree and went on to acquire a Post Graduate diploma in education / This was to fully equip her for school administration and management. A combination that has shaped her approach to running an institution built on structure, accountability, and academic excellence.

It was this multidisciplinary foundation that inspired the establishment of Treville Modern Schools: a conviction that quality education requires not just passion, but sound governance, disciplined systems, and a deep understanding of how young minds are best guided toward excellence. Her legal training instills in the school a culture of order and integrity; her background in public administration ensures efficient, transparent management; and her education qualification keeps her firmly rooted in the realities of the classroom and the needs of every learner.

Under her leadership, Treville Modern Schools has grown into a trusted name in Nung Uyo Idoro, offering quality Nursery, Primary, and Secondary education distinguished by discipline, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to producing well-rounded, academically outstanding students.

"Education is both a discipline and a responsibility. At Treville, we hold ourselves to the same standard of excellence we expect from every child who walks through our gates."
— Mrs. Aniekan Affia, ,MPA, LL.B, PGDE
